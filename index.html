Zara: A Global Fashion Powerhouse Overview Zara is one of the world’s leading fashion retailers, known for offering stylish, high-quality clothing and accessories at affordable prices. Founded in 1975 in La Coruña, Spain, by Amancio Ortega and Rosalía Mera, Zara has since become a global fashion giant, with over 2,000 stores in more than 90 countries. Business Model Zara is part of Inditex, the world's largest fashion retailer, which also owns other brands like Massimo Dutti and Pull&Bear. One of Zara’s most significant innovations is its fast fashion business model, which allows the company to bring the latest runway trends to stores in a matter of weeks. Unlike traditional retailers, Zara’s production cycle is incredibly fast, allowing it to adapt quickly to changing customer demands.
